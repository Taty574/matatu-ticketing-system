Domain Requirements: Matatu Ticketing System
1. Purpose
This section outlines the operational constraints, regulatory rules, and environmental factors inherent to Nairobi’s paratransit ecosystem that govern how the Matatu Ticketing System must operate. 

It covers compliance with the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) and Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA), integration with SACCO operational structures (such as Squad/Shift splits and daily vehicle targets), stage and route dynamics (including OTC/Koja stage behavior and surge pricing during weather/traffic disruptions), and offline connectivity realities along major corridors like Mombasa Road, Thika Road, Ngong Road, and Jogoo Road.
